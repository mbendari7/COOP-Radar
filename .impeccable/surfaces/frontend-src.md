## Scope
Whole CoopRadar web app: Find jobs (list plus details), Match my resume, Tracker, AI key, Account, Admin. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and task
Canadian co-op students scanning many postings on a laptop between classes, plus recruiters judging the build in 30 seconds. Task: find jobs they qualify for, see why, save and track, apply on the employer site.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every application is a letter you send and every job wears a postmark. Refuses the blue and grey rounded card job board: no card boxes, rows split by type size and thin rules.

OWN-WORLD: Postal red #C8281E header bar full width, airmail blue #1E3A8A for links, fits and selection, white letter paper ground, envelope security tint #EEF1F6 for the filter strip and selected row, ink #16181D. Archivo variable with condensed widths for headings and uppercase form labels, normal width for body, tabular numerals. Square corners, 1.5px ink rules, solid red primary buttons, outlined ink secondary buttons.

STORY: The student sees real jobs at once, filters to their term and province, picks one, reads why it fits and how many days are left, then applies on the employer site or saves it to the tracker.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Red bar with CONDENSED CAPS wordmark and tabs. Tinted filter strip with a big search box and filter chips. Left column job list, each row title, company, place and term, fit note in blue, and a round tilted postmark on the right with city and the day it was posted. Closing dates are often guessed because many boards give none, so the postmark carries the posted day and the closing date lives in the details panel bar. Right column details: big condensed title, company line, a ruled facts row (term, length, programs, grad year), a days left bar drawn to scale, why it fits list, red Apply button and outlined Save.

FORM: Registered Mail, position 5 on the ordered list of seven grounded candidates, seed key b40a32c2. Raises: no card boxes (type specimen), colour owns whole regions (guide map), deadline drawn to scale (dance score), list header names the range in view (lexicon). Signature move: the postmark date stamp, also used for tracker stages as cancellation stamps (StageStamp) and for match scores.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
