# Using CoopRadar

A plain guide for students and anyone who isn't a developer. No coding needed.

**CoopRadar is a free job site for co-op students.** It collects real co-op, internship and entry-level jobs from
employers' own career pages, and when you upload your resume it shows you the jobs you're actually a good fit for:
matched to your program, your graduation year and your skills, using what each job description asks for.

## 1. Open the site

If someone shared a link with you, just open it. That's all. There's nothing to install and you don't need an
account.

> On the free hosting plan the site goes to sleep when nobody has used it for a while. If the first page takes
> about a minute to load, that's why. It's quick after that.

## 2. Find jobs

The **Find jobs** page works like Indeed or LinkedIn. The look is borrowed from a Canadian post office: a red bar
at the top, plain white rows split by thin lines, and a round **postmark** on every job.

**Search bar.** Type a job title, keyword or company in the first box ("data analyst", "React", "Shopify"), pick a
province or state in the second, and click **Find jobs**.

**Quick filters** (the row under the search bar):
- **Co-op & internships** is picked by default. Switch to *Students & entry-level* to add junior jobs, or
  *All levels* for everything.
- **Canada** is the default. Switch to *United States* or *Canada and US* to include US internships and new grad
  roles (many students do a US co-op term).
- **On-site / Hybrid / Remote**: click one to show only that kind.

**All filters** opens a panel with everything else: job type (software, data, design, business...), your program
or major, your graduation year, province or state and city, date posted, term and length, company, and skills.
Every filter you turn on shows up as a small tag above the results; click its **x** to remove it, or
**Clear all**.

**Reading a job.** On a computer, click a job in the list and its details open on the right. You see a row of
facts (type, term, length, when it was posted, the deadline), a bar showing **how many days are left to apply**,
who it's for (the programs and graduation years the description mentions), the skills it mentions, and the full
description. Click **Apply on (company)'s site** to apply. On a phone, tapping a job opens its own page.

Each job in the list shows at a glance: **Co-op**, **Internship** or **New grad**, the term ("Summer 2027"),
**Grad year 2027** when the posting says which graduation years it wants, and a few of the skills. **New** means it
was posted in the last three days. The **postmark** on the right shows the city and the day the job was posted.
It turns red on the job you're looking at.

Some career pages don't give a deadline. Then the deadline is our guess and the page says so.

CoopRadar never applies for you. You always apply on the employer's own website.

---

### Term and length

Every job shows its work term, when it runs and how long it is, like **Summer 2027 · May to Aug · 4 months**, with
a small coloured dot for the season (blue for Winter, amber for Summer, orange for Fall). The length is what the job
says, so a 6 month job shows 6 months. Use the **Any term** and **Any length** menus in the strip above the results,
or **All filters**, to pick seasons and lengths. "About 8 months" also finds 6 to 9 month jobs. **Sort by** can put
the soonest term first (terms that already started go to the bottom), or the shortest or longest first. Jobs that
don't say their term or length show "Not stated".

Most employers never write the term in the post, so picking **Winter** would hide most real co-op jobs. When you
pick a term or a length, **Also show jobs that don't say their term or length** is on: jobs that say Winter come
first, then the jobs that don't say, so you still see everything you could apply to. Untick it to see only the jobs
that name the term. A term that already started, like Winter 2026 in October 2026, never counts as Winter. Pick
**100** under **Per page** at the bottom to go through lots of jobs quickly.

**Work term and graduation year are different things.** The work term is when you'd work, like Summer 2027. The
graduation year is when you finish school, like 2029. A job that asks for a graduation year shows **Grad year 2029**
on its own, and the **Graduation year** filter only hides jobs that ask for a different one.

### Get new jobs as they come in

CoopRadar checks every employer every 6 hours. When you come back, a blue bar says how many jobs for your search are
new since your last visit, with a button to show only those. **Sort by: Newly added to CoopRadar** lists the newest
first. To get new jobs without opening the site, set your filters, click **Get updates** and copy the feed link into
a feed reader (like Feedly or Inoreader) or a feed to email service (like Blogtrottr). New jobs for that exact search
then come to you.

## 3. Match your resume

Click **Match my resume** at the top. There are three short steps.

### Step 1: About you
- **Program or major**, **Degree** and **Graduating in**. Leave them on *Read it from my resume* and CoopRadar reads
  them from the education section of your resume. After you upload, a box with a blue border shows what it found, for example
  "Computer Science, Bachelor's, graduating 2028". If it got something wrong, pick the right answer.
- **Looking for**: *Co-op or internship* (most students), *Co-op, internship or entry-level*, or *Any level*.
- **Co-op term** (optional): the term you want, like "Summer 2027". Jobs for that term are ranked higher.
- **Countries**: Canada, the United States, or both.

### Step 2: What kind of job?
Pick job types (Software & web, Data & AI, Design & UX, Business & finance, and more). **If you leave this empty,
CoopRadar uses the usual job types for your program**: a Mechanical Engineering student sees hardware and
engineering jobs, a Business student sees business, product and marketing jobs, and so on. You can also add a
job title or keywords ("data analyst"), a province, and a work mode.

### Step 3: Upload your resume
Drag your resume onto the box, or click to choose it. PDF or Word, up to 5 MB.

You don't need to sign up. The site makes you a **private temporary account** that's kept for 7 days. To keep it,
click **Keep my account** in the light blue strip under the top bar and add an email and password.

### Your results
**Jobs that fit your resume** shows your 20 best matches, best first. Each one has:
- a **score out of 100**, shown in a round stamp (red for a strong match). Skills count the most. The rest comes from the job being a co-op or internship, naming
  your program, wanting your graduation year, and being for the term you picked. Only a job that fits on every
  count gets 100.
- **Why it fits**, in plain words, taken from the job description: "Internship", "Asks for Computer Science /
  Software, which fits your program", "For students graduating in 2028, like you", "Summer 2027 term, the one you
  want", "For students still in school", "4 of 5 listed skills on your resume".
- **On your resume**: the job's skills you already show. **Worth adding, if you have it**: the ones you don't.
- **View job and apply**, and **Save to tracker**.

Jobs that ask for a *different* graduation year are left out, so you don't waste time on them. Jobs that don't
mention a year stay in.

Change anything in steps 1 or 2 and click **Update my matches**. It's free and takes a second.

The **Resume feedback** tab shows, for free, the skills employers can see on your resume and the skills open jobs
ask for most that your resume doesn't show.

---

### Make your resume fit a job

Open any job, or any match, and look for **Make your resume fit this job**. It's free and needs no AI key. You get:
- quick checks, like whether your program and graduation year are ones it asks for, and whether your resume says you're free for its term
- the edits to make, in order, such as adding "Available for a 4 month Summer 2027 co-op term" near the top
- the job's skills and words, split into ones already on your resume and ones worth adding if they're true for you

Only add things you've really done. Matching is lenient on purpose: jobs that aren't a perfect fit still show up
lower down, because applying widely is how most students land a co-op.

## 4. Keep track of applications

Click the bookmark on any job (or **Save to tracker**). The **Tracker** page lets you move each job through
*Applying, Applied, Interview, Offer*, add notes and dates, see deadlines coming up this week, and export
everything to a spreadsheet. Switch between **List** and **Board**. On the board you drag a job from one column
to the next.

## 5. Optional: add a free AI key

Everything above works without AI. With your own AI key you also get a full resume review, a tailoring plan for
any job, a cover letter opener, and answers to questions like "Do I have the skills for this?".

**Free option: Google Gemini**
1. Go to **https://aistudio.google.com/apikey** and sign in with a Google account.
2. Click **Create API key** and copy it (it starts with `AIza`).
3. On CoopRadar, click **AI key** at the top, choose **Gemini**, paste the key, click **Check key**, then
   **Use this key**.

ChatGPT (OpenAI) and Claude (Anthropic) keys work too. They're pay-as-you-go, usually a few cents for a review and
a few tailoring requests. An API key is separate from a ChatGPT Plus or Claude.ai subscription.

**Is my key safe?** It stays in your browser. CoopRadar passes it to your AI provider for each request and never
saves or logs it. It's forgotten when you close the tab unless you tick **Remember on this device**.

## 6. Run it on your own computer

You only need this if you want your own copy. You need two free programs, both with normal installers:
- **Python** 3.12 or newer: https://www.python.org/downloads/ (on Windows, tick **Add python.exe to PATH**)
- **Node.js** (the LTS version): https://nodejs.org

Then:
1. On the GitHub page, click **Code**, then **Download ZIP**, and unzip it. (Or, if you use git:
   `git clone https://github.com/mbendari7/COOP-Radar.git`.)
2. Open a terminal in that folder. On Windows: open the folder, click the address bar, type `powershell` and press
   Enter.
3. Type this and press Enter:
   ```
   python start.py
   ```
4. The first time takes a few minutes: it installs everything, builds the site and creates a private database in
   a `.coopradar` folder in your home folder. Then your browser opens **http://localhost:8000**. Real jobs appear a
   few minutes later (it pulls them from about 170 employers).

Next time, `python start.py` starts in seconds. To stop it, press **Ctrl + C** in the terminal. To start over with
empty data, delete the `.coopradar` folder in your home folder.

Want it on the internet for others? See **Host it for free** in the [main README](../../README.md#host-it-for-free).

## 7. Tips

- **Put your program and expected graduation date in your resume's Education section**, for example
  "Bachelor of Applied Science, Mechanical Engineering. Expected graduation: April 2028". CoopRadar, and real
  employers' systems, read it from there.
- **Have a clear Skills section** ("Python, SQL, React, Git"). It's what gets matched first.
- **Only add skills you really have.** "Worth adding" is a learning list, not something to paste in.
- **Use a text PDF**, exported from Word or Google Docs. A scanned image can't be read.
- **Upload a new version any time.** Your choices are kept and your matches update.
- **Check back weekly.** Jobs are refreshed every few hours, and co-op roles come in waves before each term.
- **Proofread anything AI writes** before you send it.

## 8. Something not working?

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The site takes a minute to load | It was asleep (free hosting). Wait a minute. It's quick after that. |
| "The jobs didn't load" on a site you host yourself | Wait a minute and click **Try again** (the server may be waking up). If it never works, open `vercel.json` and check the `/api` line ends with `/api/:path*`, like `https://your-site.onrender.com/api/:path*`. See **Host it for free** in the [main README](../../README.md#host-it-for-free). |
| "Could not extract text" | Your PDF is probably a scanned image. Export it again from Word or Google Docs. |
| "That file is over 5 MB" | Export a smaller PDF (remove photos). |
| "We read your resume" got my program wrong | Pick the right one in **About you** and click **Update my matches**. |
| "No open jobs fit yet" | Pick more job types, add the United States, or choose *Co-op, internship or entry-level*. |
| Very few jobs on the Find jobs page | Co-op & internships in Canada is a short list between hiring waves. Try *Students & entry-level* or *Canada and US*. |
| "Too many demo accounts from this network" | Wait an hour, or make a normal account with **Sign in**. |
| My temporary account disappeared | They're deleted after 7 days. Next time click **Keep my account**. |
| An AI button says the key was rejected | The key was copied wrong or deleted. Make a new one. |

## 9. Your privacy

- Your resume is stored only in your account, and you can delete it any time (**Match my resume**, then **Delete**).
- It's only sent to an AI if you add your own key and click an AI button.
- Temporary accounts are deleted after 7 days unless you keep them.
- Jobs come from employers' public career pages. Every job links back to the employer.
